## What's New in Web Panel 1.1.25

> Assign alliance access directly from the Web Panel.

- **Assign Alliances to Admins**
  - In **Manage Admins**, expand an admin, select their **Assigned Alliances**, and click **Save Alliances**.
  - Owners and Full Access admins can assign multiple alliances to admins without Full Access. Adding or removing admins and changing permissions remain owner-only.
  - The selector includes both games when enabled and preserves assignments for disabled games. If assignments change elsewhere before you save, reload them and review your selection before saving again.
